# The XSA memory system: what is built and what to build next

Local engineering decision, starting at Emender `bde68de07`, SXGC `f1eebec`. This is a system map and a kernel qualification plan, **not** a training result or launch of a new LM. It complements [the X43 suffix-attention design, rev. 2](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x43_fork_resolved/DESIGN_SUFFIX_ATTENTION.md).

**Product:** mount one or more independently built XSAs, query them while computing, and use the results *inside* the model. A recurrent mixer is one host; a [small Transformer over a rebuilt working context](XSA_WORKBENCH_TRANSFORMER.md) is another proposed host. A session may mount a growing index of its own context, an immutable public corpus, and separately permissioned private collections simultaneously. The model also has a growable store for its own computed records. A new collection should require a manifest and a value provider, not a model fork, rebuild of other indexes, or retraining just to open it. K15 is a concrete writable controller. GDN2 is another possible host and a symmetric speed/quality comparator. No weight-level identity between the two is assumed. LZ phrases are an optional view of a source, not the storage format or a prerequisite for attention.

**Implementation direction after W3:** [the model chooses the memory program](LEARNED_MEMORY_PROGRAMS.md). The reusable platform is a controller over typed relations, reads, joins and session-local writes. W3's two-child policy is one historical control, not an architectural restriction. [P1](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x47_xsa_attention/RESULT_MEMORY_PROGRAM_PROFILE.md) connected actual live/archive span records and timed one natural batch of sampled programs through an optimizer update; the controller path was too slow at that batch geometry to admit a quality run. [P2](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x47_xsa_attention/RESULT_PACKED_PROGRAM.md) packed the same controller's register and operand tensors and checked fixed-program numerical/gradient parity. At 32 documents it reached 162 targets/s through update; P100 occurrence callbacks alone took 10.61 s of the 25.33 s batch. The full comparison remains unaffordable.

**Current research decision:** [X48](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x48_muon_continuation/RESULT.md) subsequently completed the strong direct vs optional program comparison; direct won on matched pre-update TRAIN loss and cost. [X49](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x49_live_trace_memory/PLAN.md) returns to the model's **own** searchable context. It asks first whether a live byte address can recall a useful earlier neural write, then whether learned private filing keys help. The archive is an extension, not a required source. The cost statements above describe the earlier P1/P2 stage.

## Branch-first navigation and a rewritable working context

**The primary navigation read is BRANCH, not LOCATE.** At a logical locus `(source, scope, pattern P)`, ask which byte extensions `Pb` occur and their multiplicities, with structural END distinct from all 256 bytes. A controller can keep multiple such loci, follow alternative edges, save or relax a locus, and use several responses to construct its next working context. Following a hypothetical edge in an archive does not append that byte to the observed transcript. Fetch positions only when the operation needs a particular occurrence, surrounding span, or its stored computation. B1/B2 measured the positional path; their LF-walk cost is **not a branch-query benchmark**. On a covered materialized node the child counts are already present. When keys/values are tied per outgoing symbol, weighting those distinct symbols by their counts is exact: no position enumeration is necessary, even with a learned query. It is not exact for unrelated per-occurrence neural values.

**What exists on disk:** `PILE100-REHEARSAL/pile-head100.sxi2` indexes 100,000,000,000 raw bytes, not 100 GB of pointer nodes. Its 65,533,578,480-byte v10 container includes a boundary-aware materialized upper trie (m20): 21,707,038 nodes, 551,286,102 edge entries, 2,333,923,624 bytes, depth 8, minimum support 1,000 (`PILE100-REHEARSAL-run.log`, `CONTEXT_TRIE_PUBLISHED`); m15 defines document starts. Outgoing counts at retained nodes include rare children even when the child has no materialized node. A missing materialization is not proof that a string is absent. The deeper sequence structure is represented implicitly by the compressed index; uniform-cost arbitrary child/parent/suffix-link traversal is not a capability of this artifact. The earlier P100c v6 is a different build of the 100 GB prefix.

**Two Transformer options (neither implemented by B1/B2).** With `[stable prefix | replaceable suffix]`, causal self-attention can reuse the unchanged prefix KV; positions at and after the earliest edit generally need recomputation. The [rebuilt-workbench design](XSA_WORKBENCH_TRANSFORMER.md) instead retains the history, indexes, bookmarks, and computed records but recomputes the whole small neural view. It can replace the first slot as freely as the last. It is a distinct model, not an exact shortcut for full-history dense attention. The [W1 no-training implementation](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x47_xsa_attention/RESULT_W1_WORKBENCH.md) now runs the second option with model-issued branch choices and an addressed conditional write on a four-document native fixture. CPU/GPU traces agreed; the rebuilt policy used an extra forward and did not beat direct suffix backoff. It did not run on P100. [W3's natural test](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x47_xsa_attention/RESULT_W3_NATURAL.md) did: it shared P100 branch pages and a pretrained neural backbone across matched 4.19M-target training runs. Direct retrieval won over the proposed second-successor join; batching made this measurable without millions of synchronous Python queries.

## Architecture and ownership

1. **Index registry and scope.** A source is a versioned collection: namespace, commit/content identity, corpus encoding/remap, document map, capabilities and value-schema identity. A live source also has a session and visible-prefix watermark. Open only sources allowed for the request. Filtering of documents/regions and held-out source exclusions happens **before** depth selection, support, capping, totals and normalization. An unavailable exact filter is an error, not an invitation to query globally. Membership leakage through aggregate counts matters as well as leaking retrieved text. A numerical bank ID or device row index is transient, never an authorization token.
2. **Native navigation is separate from value evaluation.** `seek/extend/relax/project`, e.g. search exact byte suffixes, grow/restrict occurrence sets, or join several anchors. Return logical `(source, version/session, doc, offset, span)` and evidence (depth/support, kernel weight, visibility), or a typed aggregate. Persist logical references rather than SA row numbers. Do not silently equate dropping leading bytes with a tree-parent step. For a live stream, append after prediction; EOF of the current prefix is not END. For a frozen archive a continuation already in an authorized document is retrievable. Boundary checks must cover both the matched region **and the read**. No sentinel byte. The readonly core and the live backend need not share on-disk layout.
3. **Values are not stored in every XSA.** Context: write the vector the model actually computed for a visible position/record. Archive: instantiate a declared provider, e.g. original-byte/region embeddings from the located span, or an independently versioned feature sidecar. Do not assume an archive of arbitrary byte strings has K15 activations. Do not mutate a shared company index with session-specific writes. Optional session overlays can associate computed values or bookmarks with logical archive addresses. Do not allocate a GPU K/V vector for every byte of a 100 GB corpus. Gather/deduplicate only an eligible working set (or exact aggregate); cache by source, encoder/value version and scope. Old learned vectors from another checkpoint are not current-state K/V.
4. **Backbone-independent device operation.** For token/head/query `q` and mounted bank `s`, define weights for eligible addressed value records `j`: `a_j = kappa(qtext,j) * exp(q·k_j / sqrt(d))`, with nonnegative suffix/region kernel `kappa`. Read `r_s = sum(a_j*v_j) / sum(a_j)`, zero on empty. Evaluate and normalize **per source**, then mix outputs with explicit source scales/routing; a billion-byte library doesn't win just by having more positions than the current document. State how multiple loci contribute (e.g. additive prior mass for the same occurrence); do not accidentally deduplicate distinct evidence, double-count clipped occurrences, or normalize different operators as though they were one. A bounded page, full interval, and exact sufficient-statistic reduction are distinct operators. Return full/returned mass or count, overflow/truncation status, and capability errors. Refuse to label an incomplete page exact.
5. **Programmable recurrence.** The eventual constructed head must issue the initial policy's requests, maintain its logical handles/registers, accumulate attention, expose the read to downstream computation and be able to write a result used by later queries. SXGC enforces exact storage/search; the device primitive does the indexed reduction. Supplying precomputed addresses to a read kernel is necessary but is **not** construction of the whole controller. Demonstrate a working policy before the first optimizer update, on both a live and an external source. After that compare hard chosen-only vs soft training paths and measure dependent reads, writes, latency and bytes moved. A host prefetch plan cannot know future *learned* queries; fixed-policy prefill can be batched, learned decode needs a measured request/return path.

## The kernel boundary and an important limit

Start with one compact device row per `(query, head, bank)`, candidate indices into a resident working-set page, log prior masses, Q and addressable K/V. Grouped rows allow simultaneous sources and ragged fanout without one Python dispatch per candidate or per bank. Fuse gather → additive log prior + dot → stable masked softmax → weighted vector read in Triton; implement the adjoint, including repeated addresses. Initialize the query projection to zero with nonzero keys: the *specified* suffix kernel then supplies the birth weights while the query projection can receive a gradient. Empty bank returns exactly zero (also zero gradient). Application-level routing needs an explicit nonzero load-bearing birth mode and a correct doors-off intervention; a permanently zero branch does not count.

At birth a count and a value sum **would suffice** for uniform weights inside each contributing interval *if they are available*. SXGC branch counts alone do **not** supply sums of arbitrary, checkpoint-dependent neural values. Fixed byte-embedding values can use an exact histogram × embedding reduction; dynamic values require maintained sums, a gather, or explicit sampling with error/cost disclosed. Arbitrary learned content scores generally invalidate count/sum shortcuts. The first kernel targets exact reads of a declared bounded page, not arbitrary dense attention in sublinear time. Index navigation and building/transporting the page may dominate; treating a Python RPC loop as the production execution path is not acceptable. Conversely, porting a 63 GB compressed mapped container to HBM without pricing it is not a design.

## Work order and acceptance

- **A: measured primitive.** Write forward/backward Triton and a separate PyTorch reference. Qualify same candidate/value tensors, empty/singleton rows, duplicates/aliasing, extreme log priors, disjoint/remapped banks, birth parity and gradients including repeated value writes. Price single-request decode and batched prefill/training; give hardware, dimensions, precision, allocations, native lookup, page build/transfer, device-only time, synchronized end-to-end wall. Report a slowdown if observed; never use CPU timing to claim GPU speed. See [A1 protocol](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x47_xsa_attention/README.md) and the [measured resident-page GPU result](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x47_xsa_attention/RESULT_RESIDENT_READ.md). The latter is not an integrated index/mixer timing.
- **B: real mounts and application.** Resolve one session and at least two separately bound immutable collections without modifying host model weights. Include separate and simultaneous reads, source removal and substitution, exact boundary/scope checks and empty/overflow behavior. Use a constructed start policy and an ordinary recurrent residual seam. Inventory unsupported capabilities explicitly. A fixture proves mounting semantics, not petascale deployment. Measure the existing P100 container only through a validated operation it actually supports. Distinguish test harness adapters from a persistent native binary batch path.
  **Verified B1 boundary:** the [mounted-read result and capability matrix](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x47_xsa_attention/RESULT_B1_MOUNTED_READ.md) show the same initialized residual head reading a native session (timed on an unsealed three-byte tail) and two whole-bank archives, F6 and rehearsal P100. A CPU-only correction verifies host slot-to-span provenance and key-specific P100 counts; the existing GPU timings predate that correction. A host-chosen threshold issued a dependent live request, not learned navigation. Partial document scope is refused. This is not a persistent archive service, a timed backbone, or an LM quality result.
- **C: decide by bottleneck.** If host search or transfer dominates, prioritize native batching/packing, immutable shards and GPU-resident hot directories/pages before cleverer neural kernels. If GPU gather dominates, consider sorting/reusing pages, tiled segmented reductions, exact aggregate kernels or structured candidate proposals. If scheduling is the problem, eliminate Python/device synchronizations from the hot loop. Published device microseconds alone answer none of these questions.
- **D: real LM training only once usable.** Keep the strong direct archive and local evidence baselines; same mounted sources, permissions, neural context, data/exposure and budgets for each host. Compare learned initialized policy vs frozen policy, matched random policy, no-index, source swap/drop and record reset. Measure ordinary all-byte NLL and compute, then delayed use. No new CPU micro-training ladder; no board, scale run or publication is part of this reset.

## B2: portable whole-archive positional reads

A compatible immutable v10 archive can now be registered by namespace and versioned [descriptor](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x47_xsa_attention/b1_mounts.json), without changing a model. The [host adapter](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x47_xsa_attention/b2_worker.py) binds one `xsa positional-worker INDEX CORPUS` child to each admitted mount. `WorkerRegistry.resolve(name, rows)` looks up each distinct byte key once **per request**, expands votes in original order, and returns `(Span(source,version,doc,start,stop), value)` pairs for B1 packing. No cross-request answer cache. It refuses partial scopes before open; this trusted local adapter is not a tenant ACL. The two-key P100 restriction belongs to the experiment, not the general API.

The [wire contract](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x47_xsa_attention/B2_PROTOCOL.md) accepts arbitrary byte patterns (1–256 bytes, at most 16 per batch), verifies matched bytes against the corpus, and uses m15 to exclude crossing spans. A raw interval above eight is refused, not truncated. The client requires both newline-terminated answer and timing records before releasing any spans; a malformed reply terminates the worker. Existing `seeds --serve` returns the same positive coordinates under full-length seed settings, but lacks byte verification, end-boundary filtering and a distinct overcap status. Branch counts and longest-MEM summaries are not positional reads.

**Where time went.** In the [paired CPU receipts](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x47_xsa_attention/receipts/b2/samples.jsonl), median per-batch wall for P100 keys 2 and 6 was 1,362 ms under the original fresh-process CLI, 1,358 ms from launch to validated response under the new one-shot worker, and 1,236 ms on the resident worker after open. These are process-cold versus process-warm, **not cold disk**. F6 pairs were 18.93, 14.94, and 0.40 ms. Key 2 alone paid 81,967 and 720 LF steps for its two hits; key 6 paid 3,801 for one. The [cache control](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x47_xsa_attention/receipts/b2/cache-samples.jsonl) reduced decodes with a larger arena but did not reduce LF steps. Price locator sampling on a separately authorized fixture rather than building a P100 sidecar. The [resident Triton measurement](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x47_xsa_attention/RESULT_RESIDENT_READ.md) starts with values and addresses already on device; do not add it to CPU request walls. Neither an integrated latency nor a novel-query throughput distribution follows from these runs. Timings predate the [client correction](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x47_xsa_attention/B2_CLIENT_REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md).
