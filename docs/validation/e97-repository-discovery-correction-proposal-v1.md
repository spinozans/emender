## Why the eight records are not admitted

Eight narrow records are useful proof that discovery/error-recovery trajectories
can be represented faithfully, but are too small to support another learning
claim. Admission therefore requires at least256 verified records across at least
eight families, split between at least128 clean-discovery and128 recovery
records. Prompts, code paths, repairs and tests must be distinct; exact templates
are capped at32 records. Every repair needs a fresh positive verifier and the
original needs a negative verifier. Protected repositories and all evaluation
trajectories remain excluded. Exact native replay, sequence/mask deduplication and
protected-overlap checks are mandatory.
