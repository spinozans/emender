Updated at the author's instruction on 2026-10-14.

The empirical argument in `paper/main.reader-first.typ` is confined to the current E97/GDN2-MLP 1.3 B pair, its actual-geometry systems measurements, and its released-checkpoint computational probes. Relevant formal statements are identified by theorem name and retain their stated hypotheses.

The author has withdrawn confidence in using the older E88/GDN1 empirical comparisons and their associated interpretations as evidence for this paper. Those benchmark tables, cross-cohort syntheses, and claims are excluded from the alternate, including its appendix. This records a confidence and evidence-scope decision; it does not diagnose a particular contamination bug or establish that any particular old number is false.

Other exploratory work, including small-scale probes and broader architecture searches, is outside this focused comparison. Exclusion from the current paper is not by itself a new validity judgment about every exploratory run.

A brief history appendix records development stages and source pointers without benchmark tables or extended interpretation. The canonical manuscript, Git history, raw results, and formal proof sources remain untouched. Their preservation is provenance, not renewed validation or endorsement.

The current S5 result is described directly: GDN2-MLP leads the long-horizon comparison under its recorded protocol, and both curves decline with length. It is not framed as a failed replication of an older model comparison. The iterated nonlinear-map advantage is evidence supporting the proposed computational bias, with the existing single-seed, stopping-rule, optimization, and formal-scope qualifications retained.
