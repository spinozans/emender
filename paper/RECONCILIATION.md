# Retirement receipt: Nonlinear Delta Memory → Emender Memory

28 October 2026 · X41 architecture-first revision

This is the retirement record for the fixed-state manuscript represented by [main.typ](main.typ), [COHERENCE_PASS_NOTES.md](COHERENCE_PASS_NOTES.md), [ndmpapernotes.md](ndmpapernotes.md) and [OUTLINE.md](OUTLINE.md). Those sources contain different historical stages; the table identifies the claim and its scope rather than treating every planning assertion as the final manuscript's conclusion. The source manuscript, released PDFs, `tmlr/` lineage and `review/` remain immutable history. The successor is [Emender Memory](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x41_unified_report/PAPER.md).

The name also changes referent. E88/E97 in the old paper are fixed matrix-state recurrences with nonlinear temporal updates. Emender Memory in X41 is the budgeted, re-addressable slot architecture with age-based correction, merge and pressure. An E97 result is not silently renamed a slot-memory result. Retirement withdraws the old narrative as the active research spine; it does not invalidate its scoped observations or discard its methods.

## Receipt and inheritance discipline

All newly quoted empirical values are read from machine receipts, not copied from the old prose. [claims.json](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x41_unified_report/claims.json) C31–C34 binds the old `docs/` and `paper/results/` evidence as well as byte-identical snapshots of the externally retained comparison/search JSONs; [provenance.json](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x41_unified_report/receipts/provenance.json) records their original paths and hashes. The numeric [validator](../experiments/expressivity_tasks/x41_unified_report/validate.mjs) checks these bindings and derived differences. Historical-only rows identify scope and disposition without asserting that X41 reran or numerically re-certified their experiments.

The inherited methods are **not retired**: CMA-ES-hardened controls, the matched-arm Pile recipe, exact token alignment, explicit windows, immutable releases and reporting of nulls. What retires is the expectation that state nonlinearity alone supplies the missing architectural capability. The new slot-memory paper carries the old null forward as motivation and as the baseline to beat.
