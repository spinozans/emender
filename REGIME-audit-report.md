### What “constant-lr SF” means

AdamWScheduleFree, CPUOffloadAdamWScheduleFree, or ScheduleFreeSRCandidate (BF16 stochastic rounding), with a constant configured LR **after any internal warmup**. Warmup-only is not annealing. `schedule.path=expected-schedule.json` means **sample/exposure order**, not LR decay. Historical `train.py` selects warmup/cosine only for ordinary AdamW; its SF `min_lr_frac` argument is inactive. Historical `train_e97_4b_pi_sft.py:build_optimizer` and the online `rl_train_step` path use SF; no external decay is identified. CPU-offload/SR precision and train-y versus saved-x are not interchangeable optimizer classes or equivalent checkpoint representations.
