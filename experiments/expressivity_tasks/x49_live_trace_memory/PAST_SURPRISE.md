# X49-U — can a one-byte record of an old forecast help the latest-write reader?

Registered 2026-11-25 UTC, after [Q closed](RESULT_QUERY.md), before U selection, fitting or scores. **Do not refine the address.** The incumbent B has strong current evidence and a 32-dimensional write computed *after* the old follower arrived. There is a different fact a writer could preserve: **how unlikely that follower was under the host just before it arrived**. A repeated, locally surprising byte might warrant different trust than one the host already expected. We do not assume the sign. This is a learned, read-only calibration of the adopted reader, not another filing policy, accuracy threshold, benchmark of a bigger archive or training of the backbone.

## Predictor and controls

Use the three same full-dose W continued-B checkpoints bound by [W](receipts/write_result.json) and [Q](receipts/query_result.json), seeds 4901, 4902, 4903; H4096 only. Freeze the entire host, B writer and readout. Start fresh in every document/window. Let `z_i` be the **bare causal host logits** after observing `x[i]`, before the live direct head or any correction. Write one unsigned byte at arrival `j>=1`:

`m[j] = Q(-log softmax(z[j-1])[x[j]])`, where `Q(s) = min(255, floor(16*max(0,s)+0.5))`.

This is an external observation **already made** at write time, compared to the forecast that **preceded** it. Never score the current target to construct a current feature. No forecast exists before the window's first byte; the one-byte slot at zero is reserved, zeroed and never read (literal followers have at least one preceding byte). One `uint8` per input slot, no data-derived bins, codebook or raw logits retained as the stored mark. Share the just-made host normalization when appropriate; pay for producing/reading marks in addition to B's ordinary record. The scalar is private computed information, not a searchable text byte.

At a hit `t`, let `j` be **B's** latest eligible literal follower, `c=x[j]`, `L` B's 256 log scores at `t`. Every predictor keeps the actual B vector at `j`, current host, direct evidence, addresses and logits. A small readout can only change the **log odds for this retrieved byte** by `delta = beta · phi`. It cannot reshuffle the relative probabilities of the other 255 bytes. Let `v = L[c] - logsumexp(L[b != c])`. Add `delta` to `L[c]`, then subtract `softplus(v+delta) - softplus(v)` from every score. This preserves B's original total mass even at machine precision error in B's log normalization. Use FP64 for log reductions, scalar statistics, coefficients and score offsets; evaluate seed/document losses in FP64. On a miss all scores are exactly B. At zero coefficients the result equals B everywhere.

Common hit features: `phi_N = [1, Q(-log P_B(c))/64, Q(-log softmax(z_t)[c])/64]`, with `-log P_B(c)=softplus(-v)`. Thus even the no-new-write control can use both current **B** confidence and current **bare-host** confidence for the candidate. Score these frozen siblings:

| Predictor | Extra feature beyond the current row | Fitted scalars |
|---|---|---:|
| B | none; unmodified latest-write predictor | 0 |
| N | none; recalibrate using `phi_N` | 3 |
| U | `m[j]/64` appended to `phi_N`: old forecast attached to the actual follower | 4 |
| S | `m[k]/64` appended instead: old forecast from B's registered deterministic same-follower-byte, same log-age-bin rotation (`rotated_payload_index`); singleton stays | 4 |

S is **trained with its own sham** as well as tested with it. Only the historical scalar is displaced, **not the neural vector or the candidate byte**. The sham need not share the literal suffix. Compute the donor from observed bytes and B's literal hit before gathering a single mark; do not enumerate alternative marks or vectors. S controls extra capacity and generic document/byte surprise; U:N asks if the mark adds to a current-confidence calibrator. U:B alone cannot isolate useful recall. No extra predictor selected after seeing scores.

## One-pass fitting, no feature store

Fit separately per seed and per readout N/U/S. Accumulate just a vector and small matrix in FP64 starting at zero. At B's unaltered hit probability `p=sigmoid(v)` add `(1[y=c]-p)*phi` to `g` and `p*(1-p)*phi*phi^T` to `H`. Use the stable equivalent `sigmoid(v)*sigmoid(-v)` and compute residuals without subtracting two numbers close to one. Construct all predictor-side features **before** the `y=x[t+1]` scoring seam. On misses add zero; count every real target in `D`. After the full fit population, solve once per seed/readout

`(H/D + (1/4096)*I) beta = g/D`.

This is **one ridge-regularized Newton step at the unchanged B predictions**, all coefficients including the intercept regularized, not a claim to have maximized likelihood. Fix the feature scale, ridge and one-step rule now; no iterations, grid, clipping after the solve or use of the probe to recalibrate. Accumulate in registered document rank order. Check finite, symmetric and positive regularized systems and the solve residual. Do not save per-target features, copy labels, addresses, candidate scores or host/mark tapes; retain only aggregate moments, counts, final coefficients and private document-level loss/work receipts. The global fit sums are not live model context. Nothing in one document can supply a neural value to another or to a different seed.
