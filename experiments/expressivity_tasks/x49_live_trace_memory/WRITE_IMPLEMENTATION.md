This implements [the registered writer comparison](WRITE_READ.md). **A correct two-hop fixture is not evidence that it improves natural prediction.** The adopted baseline is the trained B reader. P is the same-size control that recomputes the inner operand at the current writer; L retrieves that operand from an earlier writer. Only L:P isolates its source. Both are trained controls for L:B. One extra hop, no learned key, no recursively enriched operand.

## Qualification and execution

- [`write_head.py`](write_head.py), [`write_origin.py`](write_origin.py), [`coupled.py`](coupled.py): each seed supplies its own hash-bound trained B host and head; reset both optimizers. P/L add the same 7,264 parameters. No carry across documents, and no cross-weight feature cache. The scalar reference, gradient and complete same-arm successor fixtures are in [`test_write_head.py`](test_write_head.py).
- [`write_run.py`](write_run.py), [`write_panel.py`](write_panel.py): qualify actual seed origins and fresh moments on CPU. Verify each launch's host, literal and full head state. The first update's predictions and common exposure counters for each P/L must match its own seed's continued B; **do not require identical gradients or later trajectories**. Single-use W binding, TRAIN stream and paths; `relative_run` and `panel` are not W launchers. Nine training leases followed by at most one final; no retry.
- Freeze full-dose and actual common-wall checkpoints (including byte digests) before any short-test access. W weights-only milestones every 32 updates, recovery with both optimizers every 128. The registered device and allocation settings remain enforced. During the final lease, after freezing and before raw access, exercise a complete frozen L predictor on five **invented** input lengths (1, 16, 64, 65, 4095), covering both short-recurrent and chunk prefill. This is a counted technical check, not an extra natural exposure. Failure stops the run; there is no substitute kernel, model or document. The ordinary synthetic one-byte full-recompute timing is separate from natural evaluation.
- [`panel_eval.py`](panel_eval.py), [`endpoint.py`](endpoint.py), [`write_analysis.py`](write_analysis.py): one full unpadded document per prefill, one observed input byte per real target, all misses included. At full dose score three correct horizons and distinct H4096 binding interventions. Extra clock-only milestones score **only correct H4096**. An endpoint-window is one progress unit; its target count is the actual shifted length, not 4,096 and not multiplied by its number of cells. The witness checks a cumulative clock derived independently from the frozen length sequence. The primary and each paired bootstrap sample divide NLL sums by **their own real-target total**. Positive contrast = control minus candidate. Positive sham contrast = wrong minus correct.
- [`test_write_panel.py`](test_write_panel.py) exercises short source identity, zero/one hit cases, exact all-byte NLL sums, byte rather than document means, joint resampling, injected W origin/stream, bad authority, partials, mixed full/common-wall cell sets and tampered checkpoint rejection. CPU fixtures do not open a natural final, authorize a GPU, or establish a speed/quality result.
