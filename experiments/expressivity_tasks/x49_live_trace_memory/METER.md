# X49 local feasibility meter — proposed spend, **not an authorization**

Registered 2026-11-25 UTC, before any X49 card time. This is the one small measurement needed to price the registered natural comparison: can the **actual** retained GDN2 do the complete, unsegmented 4,096-target update and deterministic recovery, at what cost? CPU/tiny-host replay, 32×128 X48 timings and storage formulas are not 1×4096 device measurements.

* **Admission:** lambda01 only, one broker-selected physical GPU, **one attempt, ≤900 inclusive card-seconds total (15 card-min)** including preparation inside the lease, compilation, warmup, checkpoint/restore/hashing, probes, teardown and failure. This number is a request, not a balance. Require a new explicit operator authorization bound to the qualified source. No waiting in the broker, auto-retry, extra card, fallback host, X48 funds, remote run or reservation of the subsequent natural panel. Refusal before grant is not a completed device probe. Missing release proof is unknown, not a zero charge.
* **Work cap:** sequential A, B, F, C starting afresh at the same X48 direct H endpoint, seed 4901. For each arm use the same three deterministic synthetic 4,097-byte documents; exactly three ordinary full-window updates and at most one repeated successor after checkpoint restoration. **16 physical updates maximum**, 4,096 shifted synthetic targets each. No natural TRAIN, calibration or final bytes, no model selection. Time step 1 separately from 2/3. Save and hash one real FP32 weights-only milestone and one complete optimizer recovery checkpoint per arm at step 2. After step 3 restore step 2, repeat step 3 and require identical scores, work, host/head weights and hybrid optimizer states. Failure or nonfinite/OOM/timeout stops the attempt; no smaller geometry substituted. No state from the meter seeds natural training.
* **Inference cost sample:** after replay, one new synthetic window per arm at fixed weights, one fresh full causal host prefill. Reuse only that same window/weight packet across H=128/1024/4096 and the H=4096 literal-only (B/F/C) and trace-only (F/C) shams. Paired calls compute the donor maps identically; change only the payload binding. Measure host and head wall separately. This is teacher-forced analysis cost, **not** autoregressive service latency or a natural evaluation. No quality inference from these synthetic scores.
* **Resource checks:** 16 GiB free on the actual output filesystem before admission and 4 GiB minimum before each arm's artifacts. No more than 80% of visible device memory; strict Torch deterministic mode, FP32 masters, BF16 host, four CPU threads, same allocator and cuBLAS settings as X48. Start the broker wrapper via detached `Popen`, stdin DEVNULL, retained private logs. Check PID/starttime and PPid 1 before acquisition. Enforce timeout from actual lease creation with a teardown margin; independently witness at least two different actual optimizer-step counts under that owner and at least one renewed heartbeat, check terminal release and charge to whole seconds inclusive. Time/disk/owner limits are not relaxed if a probe is slow. All paid partials count.
* **Output/decision:** private per-step performance, prefill/head/ckpt/reload timings, resource peaks, consistency digest and terminal inclusive cost. Only aggregate measurements/digests may be published. Price a subsequent panel separately including three seeds, 32-step analysis checkpoints, recovery cadence, common-wall endpoints, calibration/final evaluation and headroom. Synthetic density and warm timings do not bound real natural costs. No natural lease follows automatically; this meter by itself cannot nominate a model for Lux.
