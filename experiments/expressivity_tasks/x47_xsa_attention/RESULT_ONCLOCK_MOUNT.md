# S19: descriptor reuse shortened the queue, but missed the on-clock serving gate

**Keep fresh verified mounts as the default for this offer-before-admission service.** Reusing the checked document-boundary image improved whole-trial wall and offer-to-first p95 on both hosts. It did **not** clear all registered thresholds: replicated lifecycle and charged setup missed; the retained host's first group waited **1.300692×** as long at p95 (limit 1.30). No rounding rescue, no rerun. [S16](RESULT_PINNED_DESCRIPTOR.md)'s metadata-only recommendation on its CPU workload is unchanged.

The [protocol](ONCLOCK_MOUNT_PROTOCOL.md) starts the primary clock at the first **actual** insertion into the bounded descriptor FIFO. All eight first offers arrive before ordinary archive verification or first image construction. Only after the prior grant retires can the next group obtain its own fresh m20 process, initially cold reference LRU, scoped positional/value companion and eight private bindings. The other 24 offers arrived during mount setup; the FIFO peaked at 24 in all eight trials. No queued client already had a host slot, private tape or permission to query. Wall ends after final grant/worker and optional image close, stale-handle probes and device synchronization. Models and the size-specific graph arena are prepared once, separately charged below; whole-machine caches and load are not controlled.

**Why these are different clocks.** An image trial paid .393/.726 s for construction on replicated, .414/.813 s on retained. The first group cannot amortize that work; later groups can. Complete trial seconds, chronological: replicated fresh-A/image-A/image-B/fresh-B **6.553/5.785/6.212/7.052**; retained image-A/fresh-A/fresh-B/image-B **4.061/5.623/5.606/4.473**.
