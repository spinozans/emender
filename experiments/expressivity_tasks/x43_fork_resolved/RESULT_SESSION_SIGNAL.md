# Live XSA: cost to serve and evidence past 256 bytes

The exact index supplied useful byte evidence on all 32 calibration documents. This is a fixed lookup result, not learned use or neural NLL. In `PREREGISTRATION_SESSION_SIGNAL.md`'s 57,344 paired targets, local unigram, short lookup, full lookup and rotated-full scored **3.3260 / 3.1530 / 2.9538 / 3.5740** nats/byte. Full vs short: **−0.1993**; top-1 29.09% vs 23.12%. An eight-byte suffix had observed continuations at 19.55% vs 10.45% of positions; 14.51% of targets had at least one additional occurrence beyond the bounded counts. Rotating labels hurt. Only within-document prefixes through 2047 preceding bytes were tested. Documents were length-filtered, not population-representative; no final test accessed.

Systems measurements, separate from the evidence pilot: EPYC 7713, single session client, release Rust binary, `cmake --help-full` (3.28.3) as one technical document; repeating 8-byte motif as a second workload. At 100K/500K/1M technical-text bytes: warm append+query p99 81/79/76 microseconds over 511 observations per size, including two pipe round trips and client parsing. At 1M: 13.1 ms bulk ingest (no per-byte reads); 463.5 ms worst of two synchronous seals; 7.6 ms cold first read; server high water 79.7 MiB. Repetitive input at 1M: p99 71 microseconds, worst seal 61.6 ms. These are observed samples, not bounds. Bulk ingest is **not** batched causal scoring.

Background publication, new binary: at 1M, submission took 0.23 ms; while replacement was building the client served 6,448 more append/query pairs before observing publication at 477 ms. Pair p50/p99/max 0.071/0.086/1.710 ms; high water 85.7 MiB. Polling status every 16 steps; observed completion includes polling delay. Old generation was already warm at submission. No claim about cold-start unsealed-tail speed, an always-bounded tail, repeated high write rates, or end-to-end decoder latency. Snapshot still copies text and rebuilds whole history. Rust tests cover join and END races; CLI oracle replay checked all 257 counters in 1128 replies. See `receipts/xsa_session_{latency,publication,signal}.json` for hashes and individual rows.
