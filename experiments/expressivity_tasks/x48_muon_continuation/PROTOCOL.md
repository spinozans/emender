This is not Muon versus AdamW; the optimizer family is common. No fixed-choice attribution unless separately authorized after this comparison. No development, calibration, confirmation or final-test reads/scores. Primary comparison is predictive loss on *new training inputs before their update*, not held-out quality. No S21 or benchmark retries.

**Data frozen before extraction:** use the existing hash-bound flagship raw-byte manifest (48b9095e…), train only, offsets ≥100,600,000,000, length ≥129, one window per unique document SHA. Exclude all W3 panel document SHAs and the historical manifests named by its verified `panels.json` (60144b7c…). Order by SHA256(`x48-muon-v1:` + document SHA). Take 32,768 documents; choose aligned window `int(sha[16:32],16) % floor((length-1)/128)` within each fully hash-matched document via C14 window semantics. Use B32×128; both arms get the exact same 1,024 batches; no padding, cross-window state, replay of targets on the main trajectory, or future addresses. Source-disjoint from P100; not a claim that duplicate passages never occur. Keep raw windows/addresses/checkpoints private.
