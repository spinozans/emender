## a-phase vs b-phase

The phases do NOT differ: both are exactly 0.5 in every bin and every band
word. The recorded trace alternates strictly per token in every head — the
case prefix (word 0, head 0) is [R-EMPTY, R-CONFLICT, R-NOMATCH, R-CONFLICT,
R-NOMATCH, ...] in every bin: SOS appends, the first phase token CORRECTS the
SOS row, the next token appends (no age-1 row exists after a correction —
corrections do not renew birth), the next corrects that fresh append, and so
on through BOTH phases unbroken; EOS corrects the last append. Per word/head
at k=128: 129 appends (1 R-EMPTY + 128 R-NOMATCH) and 129 corrections (128
phase tokens + EOS). This is the F12g adjacency-addressed mechanism operating
exactly as trained; the alternation IS the counting semantics, not noise.
Every 64-token chunk therefore contains ~32 correction events and 64
intra-chunk matched-value references; the reasons histogram is
correction_or_cap + write_after_append on 100% of chunks, and
MUTATION_CANDIDATE is 0% everywhere — even the weaker append-only
certificate never fires at C=64. Maximal entry-pure segments are 1-2 tokens
long.

## Verdict (registered bands, RECEIPTS.md addendum)

The trained ladder event-token fraction is 0.5 on every bin, every phase,
every depth — the HIGH band (>= 40%), indistinguishable from the initialized
cell's 50% (part 1). The answer to the open question is: NO, the untrained
50% was not initialization noise. Correction-every-other-token IS the
trained counting semantics on the curriculum's target task; the sequential
core is intrinsic to the trained instrument. Speculative chunk-parallel
