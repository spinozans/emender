# Why the all-frontier GPU meter stopped, and the offline correction

The [original meter](../CORPUS_DEVICE_RESULT.md) failed one of its 12 real-device tests. It did not reach the update clock. No retry, alternate shape, smaller semantic leaf, or tolerance change was used to claim a pass.

**Diagnosis.** For `N=205, rank=33, value_width=9, leaf=201`, `_bvalue` rounded the row axis up to 256 and attempted to produce every output row of the leaf in one program. The RTX 6000 Ada rejected its **102,912-byte** shared-memory request; limit **101,376**. Offline assembly of the *original* source, including the 16-byte pointer specialization from the launch, produced the same cubin SHA-256 as the rejected runtime binary (`3ae531ca657a2822863041ef42f60fe4030bb6dae7cb4844c475b5e01b6a9042`) and the same `metadata.shared`. Successful assembly is not a launch test.

**Change.** Keep the forward normalization over **all 201 rows**. Once the per-leaf peak, reciprocal denominator and mean are saved, the score and value derivatives for one row do not depend on the derivatives of another row. Dispatch disjoint subtiles of at most **64 output rows** in the third grid dimension of both backward kernels. Each row still uses the original full-leaf statistics. The 201-row leaf has four subtiles; mask the tail and the final partial leaf, and store each gradient once. No new reduction, atomics, or change to the attention law.

**Evidence with CUDA hidden.** 30 ordinary CPU / Triton-interpreter tests passed, including the original failing numerical fixture with its original tolerances, a 513-row / 256-row-leaf VJP, disjoint row coverage and rejection of the old over-limit metadata. `codegen.py` now checks `metadata.shared` against the metered hardware limit, including the actual asymmetric geometry and aligned-pointer variants. On that geometry the corrected `_bvalue` needs **29,184 bytes**, `_bscore` **8,192**, and the unchanged full-leaf forward **49,152**. The largest request among 66 checked variants (49 distinct cubin hashes) was 65,536 bytes. [Compact evidence](receipts/subrow_resource.json).

This addresses the specific resource bound in offline code generation. It is **not** evidence that the corrected CUDA launch ran, that every possible specialization fits, or that the all-frontier training update is faster on hardware. The failed admission remains spent and the real-device clock is still missing.
